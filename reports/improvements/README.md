# Improvement proposals

Track evidence-backed usability, performance and scalability opportunities here when a defect has not been established. Confirmed or suspected defects belong in [reports/bugs](../bugs/README.md). A proposal is not a failed test or a confirmed root cause.

Use `IMP-NNN-short-description.md`. Include status, affected route/journey, evidence and its source (measured, inspected or user-reported), potential impact, proposed investigation or change, unknown requirements, and a validation plan. Keep measurements and screenshots sanitized and local under the exploration evidence directory. Link a related bug rather than duplicating it; if investigation establishes a defect, create or link its bug report and update the proposal.
